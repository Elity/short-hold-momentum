RADIOSHACK CORPORATION - SYMBOL CHANGE (RSH) #36153 DATE:   FEBRUARY 3, 2015 SUBJECT: RADIOSHACK CORPORATION - SYMBOL CHANGE OPTION SYMBOL: 2-3-2015 RSH remains RSH 2-4-2015 RSH becomes RSHC NEW SYMBOL: RSHC DATE: 2/4/15 The Options Clearing Corporation (OCC) has been advised that Radioshack Corporation (RSH) will change its trading symbol to RSHC, effective February 3, 2015, due to the listing of the company on an OTC market. As a result, option symbol RSH will change to RSHC effective at the opening of business on February 4, 2015. Strike prices and all other option terms will not change.
